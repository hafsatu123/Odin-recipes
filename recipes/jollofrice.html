Jollof Rice with Roasted Meat

HOME

This is a tasty, appetizing, delicious and an affordable Dish.

It has a simple recipe. And doesnot take much time to prepare.

It requires stew preparation before the rice is prepared.

You can decide to serve with fried plantain as shown in the Picture above.

Now, let's talk about the ingrediants and recipe.

LIST OF INGREDIANTS

  • Fresh Tomatoes and Pepper
  • Fresh Onions
  • Cooking Oil
  • Rice
  • Salt and Spices
  • fish
  • Meat

HOW TO PREPARE THE DISH

  1. Roast the meat on fire until well cooked.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, onions and pepper and prepare stew with the spices.
  3. Add water and allow to boil.
  4. Wash and add rice when it begins to boil.
  5. Wash and add fish, onion and salt.
  6. Close and allow to cooked then check after some few minutes
  7. Reduce heat then allow the vapour to completely cook the food
  8. Serve the food.

Enjoy your delicious meal.
